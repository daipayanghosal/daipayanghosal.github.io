Standard CVSS base scores weight severity in isolation, overlooking whether a vulnerability is actively exploited in the wild or segmented behind internal controls. VulnInvestigator computes contextual risk rankings from CVSS, CISA KEV status, asset criticality, and network exposure telemetry, correlated directly with MITRE ATT&CK STIX 2.1 techniques.
